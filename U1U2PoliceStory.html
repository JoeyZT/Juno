1. Going on a trip（旅行）

check in at the airport — 在機場辦理登機
buy souvenirs — 購買紀念品
meet relatives — 會見親戚
write postcards — 寫明信片
take photos — 拍照
make videos — 拍影片
eat local food — 吃當地食物
We need to check in at the airport two hours before the flight.
我們需要在起飛前兩小時在機場辦理登機。
I want to buy souvenirs for my friends when I visit Japan.
我去日本時想購買紀念品送給朋友。
During the holiday, I will meet relatives in my hometown.
假期期間，我會回老家會見親戚。
My grandma likes to write postcards to her friends when she travels.
奶奶旅行時喜歡寫明信片給朋友。
Don’t forget to take photos of the beautiful sunset.
別忘了為美麗夕陽拍照。
My brother likes to make videos of his trips and share them online.
哥哥喜歡把旅行拍影片並分享到網上。
When you travel, it’s fun to eat local food.
旅行時吃當地食物很有趣。

2. My travel experience（旅行經歷）

go on a boat trip — 乘船旅行
rides — 遊樂設施
meet cartoon characters — 見卡通人物
watch a parade — 觀看巡遊
take photos with koalas — 和樹熊拍照
kangaroos — 袋鼠
visit a palace — 參觀皇宮
castle — 城堡
cave — 洞穴
ride a horse — 騎馬
camel — 駱駝
feed animals — 餵動物
sheep — 綿羊
deer — 鹿
dolphins — 海豚
We will go on a boat trip around the island tomorrow.
明天我們會繞島乘船旅行。
The kids loved all the rides at the theme park.
孩子們喜歡主題公園所有遊樂設施。
My sister was happy to meet cartoon characters at Disneyland.
妹妹在迪士尼很開心見卡通人物。
We went to watch a parade on the main street.
我們去大街上觀看巡遊。
Many tourists want to take photos with koalas in Australia.
許多遊客想在澳洲和樹熊拍照。
Kangaroos are very common in Australia.
袋鼠在澳洲很常見。
We will visit a palace in Europe next summer.
明年夏天我們會去歐洲參觀皇宮。
There is an old castle on the hill.
山上有一座古老城堡。
We explored a dark cave on our trip.
旅行時我們探索了一個黑暗洞穴。
Would you like to ride a horse on the farm?
你想在農場騎馬嗎？
We saw many camels in the desert.
我們在沙漠看到許多駱駝。
Children love to feed animals at the zoo.
小朋友喜歡在動物園餵動物。
There are many sheep on the green hills.
青山上有許多綿羊。
We saw a deer in the forest.
我們在森林看見一隻鹿。
We watched dolphins swim and jump in the sea.
我們看見海豚在海裡游和跳。

3. Police stories（警察故事）

steal — 偷竊
shoot at the robber — 向劫匪開槍
escape — 逃跑
chase — 追趕
cry for help — 呼救
arrest — 逮捕
set off the alarm — 觸發警報
Someone tried to steal my bag on the bus.
有人在公車上想偷竊我的袋子。
The police had to shoot at the robber to stop him.
警察必須向劫匪開槍阻止他。
The prisoner tried to escape from jail.
囚犯試圖從監獄逃跑。
The police had to chase the thief through the streets.
警察在街上追趕小偷。
When she saw the fire, she started to cry for help.
她看見火災時開始呼救。
The police will arrest anyone who breaks the law.
警察會逮捕任何違法的人。
The burglar set off the alarm when he tried to open the window.
竊賊開窗時觸發警報。
